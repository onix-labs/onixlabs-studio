Remove “{{ removeLabel() }}”?

Its directory moves to the Trash, including any uncommitted work. Commits that were pushed remain on the remote.

Convert to worktrees?

The folder becomes a worktree container: everything in it moves into a worktree directory, and further worktrees of the same repository can then work in parallel — each on its own branch, with its own terminals, agent, and debugger. The view reloads around the container; the path you open stays the same.
